The Strived story

Strive is a
daily practice.

Strived Sportswear started in Bacolod City with a sketchbook, a borrowed sewing machine, and a belief that athletic gear should feel as individual as the people wearing it.

Founder Kai Mendoza designs every piece to give local teams and independent athletes a louder way to show up. We make fewer, better things — built to move through hard sessions, long seasons, and every comeback.
